# Deviations from the frozen protocol (dated log)

Each row records what changed, when, why, and whether the change was made **after** seeing any test-year results. The paper reproduces this table.

| # | Date (UTC) | Change | Reason | After seeing test results? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-11-16 | **C1 under temporal splits (S0/S1)** is the unit's mean log1p(cases) over the years *before* the first training target year (Brazil 2014–17). It is identical for training and test rows. The old definition (mean of the training targets) remains available as `c1_period="train"`. | The old definition leaked each training row's own target into its feature, so C1 scored 0.263 vs 0.175 for plain L3 at the 2019 selection origin. | Seen at the 2019 *selection* origin only, not the test years. |
| 2 | 2026-11-16 | **One shared LightGBM hyperparameter set per country**, tuned once at the 2019 origin on L1, is used for all levels and controls. Per-level tuning (the original protocol) becomes a sensitivity analysis. | The 2019 tuning grid was nearly flat (validation differences ≤0.07%). It gave L1/L2 127 leaves and L3/L4 31 leaves. On the Brazil S1 test years, L3 with 127 leaves beat L3 with 31 at every horizon (Holm p ≤ 0.012). That capacity effect was 5–20× the embedding effect and confounded the ladder. | **Yes** (Brazil S1 test years). |
| 3 | 2026-11-16 | **S2 = grouped leave-states-out, 5 folds** (seeded, balanced by unit count) instead of leave-one-state-out (27 Brazil / 33 Colombia folds). Brazil's **S3 has 10 blocks grouped into 5 folds** (buffer = union). The old definitions are kept as S2L/S3B. | Compute: S2 with 27 folds was 54% of the total. Held-out units are still in states never seen in training. | No (spatial splits not run yet). |
| 4 | 2026-11-16 | Spatial splits (S2–S5) score **horizons {1, 4}** only; S1 keeps h1–h4. **S0 dropped.** Final C\* runs use **1 seed** (selection uses 3 seeds for C3/C4; the protocol said 10). No re-tuning per split. | Compute. In Brazil S1 the selection seed SD was ~0.0001 log-WIS. | No. |
| 5 | 2026-11-16 | **C1 in S2/S3 uses "matched" leave-out:** a training unit's kNN encoding excludes its own state (S2), or neighbours within D km (S3). | With plain LOO, training units' neighbours were much closer than held-out units' (median 23 km vs 105–394 km), so train and test encodings had different distributions. | No. |
| 6 | 2026-11-16 | **S5 hyperparameters** are the L1 params of the largest training country (Brazil, or Colombia when Brazil is held out). | S5 trains on pooled countries, and there is no S5-specific tuning origin. | No. |
| 7 | 2026-11-16 | **LST outlier screen** for Colombia, Peru and Taiwan: an LST value more than 10 °C below the unit's calendar-month median from years ≤2017 is set to NaN. It is OFF for Brazil runs (it flags only 0.08/0.02% of Brazil unit-months). | Cloud-contaminated cold MODIS LST (Colombia minimum −42 °C). A QC-bit mask (v2) did not remove these values and was rejected. | No. |
| 8 | 2026-11-16 | **Added a secondary "no case-history" arm** (N-levels), pre-registered in `docs/methods/nohist_arm.md` before it ran. | With case history, neither the embeddings nor the ID controls help (Brazil and Peru S1), so the ID-vs-genuine question cannot be told apart there. | **Yes** (motivated by the S1 nulls); labelled exploratory. |
| 9 | 2026-11-16 | **Equivalence framing, stated before any spatial-split result exists:** the smallest effect of interest is **1% of WIS(L3)**, the same as the protocol's ID-share gate. Each estimand is classed as equivalent / inconclusive / superior by TOST on 90% CIs. A minimum-detectable-effect table is reported per country × split. | A null result needs an explicit margin. | Partly: Brazil S1 and Peru S1 results were already seen. |
| 10 | 2026-11-16 | **Arm deferred to future work:** the TimesFM+LightGBM residual hybrid, the monthly replication arm, PDFM (L5) and the reporting-lag sensitivity. | Time and scope for arXiv v1. | No. |
