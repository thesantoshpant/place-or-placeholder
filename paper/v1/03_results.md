### Primary family: what embeddings add beyond a location control

Each contrast is a percentage of WIS(L3) on the same rows; positive values mean the added input improves the forecast. The **excess gain** (primary) is the improvement from adding embeddings to a model that already has the validation-selected location control (C\*). The **embedding gain** adds embeddings with no control, and the **identity gain** adds the control alone. These are separate comparisons, not parts of one sum.
